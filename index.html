Jayla Cockerham

Jayla’s life began in a small town called Marianna, Florida (an hour west of Tallahassee). She was born and raised there. In this town, it seemed as if everyone knew each other. The places for her and her friends to hang out in Marianna were Sonic, Walmart, the local Mexican restaurant, and the park. If not those options, they rode on Side-by-Sides, Four-Wheelers, or went out of town. This provides some insight into the environment where Jayla grew up.

Jayla’s family is originally from Miami, Florida, so she has spent several summers there with them. Her life has always consisted of family, church, and school. She has one sister whom she is very close to. They are three years apart. She is very close to her mother and is thankful for her love and guidance. She was raised in Christianity and still holds those values today. Her faith is a constant in her life. Her main interests/hobbies are music, fishing, gardening with her grandmother, and spending time with loved ones. She purchased a guitar about eight months ago and has been teaching herself in her free time. Jayla also enjoys singing.

She sings at her church in her hometown. Music has been a huge interest of hers since she was little. Jayla enjoys most movies/shows if they involve singing. She goes fishing with her uncle and sister back home. Her grandmother has played a huge role in her life. She enjoys spending time with her and caring for her plants. Jayla is a recent high school graduate. She dual-enrolled at her local community college, Chipola College. She was able to earn 50+ college credits before her high school graduation. Now, Jayla is a student at Florida A&M University pursuing a degree in Information Technology. She takes her education seriously and believes FAMU will prepare her for her future in the professional world.
