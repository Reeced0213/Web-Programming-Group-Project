How To:

      

Move Pacman Use your arrow keys to move Pacman across the game map!

      5.             
          

    Eat the Pellettes Move Pac-Man around the maze and eat the pellets to earn points.

          

    Avoid the ghosts Avoid the ghosts! If a ghost catches Pac-Man, you lose a life.

          

    Eat the Super Pellettes to temporarily turn the ghosts blue. You can then eat the ghosts for extra points.

          

    Goal: Eat all of the pellets in the maze while avoiding the ghosts and try to get the highest score possible!
