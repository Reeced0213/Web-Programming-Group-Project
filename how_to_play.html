How To Play Pacman

Move Pacman Use your arrow keys to move Pacman across the game map!

  1. Up Arrow Key
  2. Down Arrow Key
  3. Left Arrow Key
  4. Right Arrow Key

Eat the Pellets Move Pac-Man around the maze and eat the pellets to earn points.

Avoid the ghosts If a ghost catches Pac-Man, you lose a life.

Eat the Super Pellets to temporarily turn the ghosts blue. You can then eat the ghosts for extra points.

Goal: Eat all of the pellets in the maze while avoiding the ghosts and try to get the highest score possible!
