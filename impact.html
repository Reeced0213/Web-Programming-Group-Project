Pac-Man's Impact

Longevity

Since Pac-Man's release the game has become a global phenomenon. In the 1980s it sold over 350,000 copies and became the most popular game in the world dethroning games like Asteroids and Space Invaders. The game traversed through all gaming consoles even making its way to new age technology with laptops and mobile devices. Pac-Man merch was soon becoming a big success with Pac-man themed toys, clothes, chalkboards, pillows, erasers, bubble pipes, costumes, shower curtains, pens, jewelry, lunchboxes, bumper stickers, and books. There are even songs and tv shows inspired from the game. Jerry Buckner and Gary Garcia 1982 single Pac-Man Fever and the 2013 television show Pac-Man and the Ghostly Adventures. Pac-Man is also featured in many films such as Pixars 2015 and Wreck-It-Ralph 2012. Pac-Man impact and influence spans over decades and trends. Even different versions of the game began to release such as Pac-Man Plus and Baby Pac-Man but the most notable creation being Ms. Pac-Man.
