Pac-Man’s Impact

Longevity

Since its release, Pac-Man has become a global phenomenon. In the 1980s, it sold over 350,000 copies and became one of the world’s most popular games. It later appeared on consoles, computers, and mobile devices. Pac-Man merchandise, music, television, and film helped the character remain familiar across generations.

Arcade Success

Pac-Man rose above other popular arcade titles, including Asteroids and Space Invaders.

Pop Culture

The character inspired merchandise, the 1982 song Pac-Man Fever, television shows, and film appearances.

New Versions

Spin-offs such as Ms. Pac-Man, Pac-Man Plus, and Baby Pac-Man extended the original maze-game idea in different ways.

Sequels and Spin-offs

Ms. Pac-Man

  • Introduced four maze layouts that changed as players advanced.
  • Added moving bonus fruit and two warp tunnels.
  • Updated the ghost behavior from the original game.

Pac-Man Plus

  • Changed some gameplay details, including a ghost that could remain unaffected by the power pellet.
  • Updated visuals, changing the maze color from blue to teal and altering fruit designs.

Baby Pac-Man

  • Combined maze gameplay with a pinball machine.
  • Bonus fruit and pinball targets added another way to earn points.
  • Players completed rounds by clearing the dots while avoiding increasingly aggressive ghosts.
